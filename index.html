Your ship.
Your story.

Homelancer Digital is a space adventure you play right in your browser, on a phone or a computer. Take missions, earn credits, fit new weapons on your wings, and decide who you fight for.

Beyond the horizon

Chart the galaxy.

The galaxy is a fog-of-war grid. Systems, their planets and their stations appear on your chart only once you have been there.

  • Three kinds of gateJump gates, warp gates and rifts — each drawn in its own colour on the chart.
  • Fog of warUnvisited systems stay hidden until you arrive; the chart fills in as you fly.
  • Stations to dockEach system has its own stations, with dealers, repairs and a mission board.
Built for adventure

Ships & wings.

From light fighters to heavy freighters — and every faction's own fleet. Each wing carries two mounts — a shoulder on top, near the core, and a tip underneath — for guns, rail weapons and missile pods.

Swipe to explore the fleet →

Ships rendered from the game's own 3D models. Your ships first, then the factions' fleets.

Ship to mech

Transform.
Build your mech.

One tap and your ship becomes a mech that walks, digs and fights on the ground. Every mech is three parts — arms, core and legs — and you can mix them from any frame you own. Every faction fields its own mech, and its bosses are bigger.

Swipe through the mech frames →

  • ArmsAlways a matched pair. They set your gun power.
  • CoreThe upper body, with its armour and anything mounted on it.
  • LegsThe lower body. They set how fast you move.

Mech images are rendered from the game's own 3D models.
