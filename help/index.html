How it works

  • Report once, vote often. Before you post, the form shows similar reports. If yours is there, vote for it instead.
  • Statuses: New Report → Under Review → Confirmed → Fix in Progress → Fixed → Released in Update (or Unable to Reproduce).
  • Fix order: game-breaking bugs first, then multiplayer problems, major gameplay issues, visual issues, then feature suggestions.
  • Your privacy: no account or email needed. Screenshots and video links go only to the developer. Your name is optional and is the only thing shown.
  • Be kind. Spam and abuse are removed. Flag anything that breaks the rules.
  • Final say: community feedback guides the priorities; the developer keeps final creative control.
  • Free: donations are never required to report, comment, vote or test.
